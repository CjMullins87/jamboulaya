# Jamboulaya *(Seafood Allergy Friendly Version)*

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Zatarain's Jambalaya Box Mix (family size) | 1 box |
| Onion, yellow | 1 unit |
| Celery stick | 1 unit |
| Red bell pepper | 1 unit |
| Garlic | 2–4 cloves |
| Savoie's Andouille Sausage | ½ lb |
| Chicken thighs, bone-in + skin-on | 1.5–2 lbs |
| Green onions | 1 bunch |
| Cajun seasoning, additional | 2–3 tbsp |
| Water | Per package instructions |

---

## Instructions

1. **Prep work**
   - Coin the sausage. Cutting on a bias gives more surface area for browning — recommended.
   - Dice the onion, celery, and red bell pepper.
   - Dice the garlic and set it aside on its own.
   - **Leave the skin on the chicken thighs.** Pat them dry.
   - Measure out water per package instructions.
   - Get a large enough dutch oven onto the stove.
   - Dice the green onions.
   - Set oven to **350°F**.

2. Brown the sausage in the dutch oven — no extra fat needed.
   - If extra fat is needed, bacon fat is recommended.

3. Remove the sausage before it burns and set aside.

4. Add in the onion, celery, and bell pepper mixture.
   - Add a pinch of salt here to help the veggies soften sooner and release their water.

5. Cook over medium heat until translucent and tender.
   - Add extra fat if necessary to prevent sticking to the bottom.

6. Add in the garlic, stirring until cooked but not burnt.

7. Deglaze the pan by adding in your water, gently scraping the bottom to release the fond.

8. Add in the seasoning packet from the box with the rice. Raise heat to medium-high.

9. Add in the browned sausage with any accumulated fat and stir.

10. Add your personal seasoning mix — go crazy.

11. Leave on heat until a gentle simmer is going.

12. Add in the raw chicken thighs.

13. Move to the oven and cook for approximately **30–50 minutes**.
    - You're looking for: **(a)** the rice has absorbed all or most of the gravy, and **(b)** the chicken thighs are fully cooked.
    - The chicken thighs will render their fat and collagen into the gravy — you're essentially making a chicken stock and cooking it into the rice simultaneously.

14. Remove from oven once done and set aside.
    - The pot is full of food so it won't get cold for a long time.
    - You can leave the pot uncovered if you'd like to let steam out.

15. Remove the chicken thighs and set aside. Let rest for **10–15 minutes**.

16. Debone the chicken thighs.
    - Peel off the skin.
    - After a ~40 minute wet cook, the thighs should fall apart and be easy to pull into shards by hand.

17. Add the thigh meat back into the pot and fold it in.

18. Add the green onions into the pot and fold them in.

19. Serve.
